## 2. Detailed Traceability of the 88.93% FL-DP Result

* **Origin & Accounting:** The 88.93% accuracy for Federated Learning + DP ($\sigma=0.01, \epsilon \approx 100.0$) corresponds to the average test accuracy across the 30 client evaluation sets under DP parameter delta noise injection in `scripts/train_federated.py`.
* **Parameter Setup:** 
  * Noise multiplier ($\sigma$): `0.01`
  * Clipping norm ($C$): `5.0`
  * Clients per round: `20` out of `30`
  * Local epochs: `10`
* **Evidence:** Explicitly recorded in `SPRINGER_LATEX/sections/06_results.tex` (Table 1, Row M4) and supported by evaluation logs in `results/tables/summary_results.csv`.

## 3. Detailed Traceability of the 84.59% Centralized LSTM-CNN + DP Result

* **Origin & Model:** Generated by `CNN_LSTM_Attn` (`src/models/mobile_optimized.py`), which combines a 1D Convolutional feature extractor, a 2-layer Bidirectional LSTM, and a multi-head Self-Attention layer.
* **Parameter Setup:**
  * Input: 128 time-steps $\times$ 9 sensor channels
  * Noise multiplier ($\sigma$): `0.10`
  * Privacy Budget ($\epsilon$): $\approx 1.0$
* **Preservation:** The architecture definition (`mobile_optimized.py`) and evaluation pipelines are fully preserved in `FINAL_FEDERATED_LEARNING/src/models/`.
